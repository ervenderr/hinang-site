How do I plot my schedule?

Pick a shift under the calendar, then tap the days. Tap a plotted day again to remove it. Use the eraser at the top left to clear a day.

How do I set my pay?

Open More, choose your pay type (hourly, per shift, bi-weekly or monthly), your currency and overtime rules. Reports then shows your earnings.

How do I share my schedule?

Open More and tap Share schedule file or Share as calendar (.ics). Guests can open the file in Hinang or add the calendar to any calendar app.

Is my data backed up?

Your schedule lives only on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iPhone backups, but deleting the app deletes the data. Use Share schedule file to keep a copy.

Does Hinang sync between devices?

Not yet. To move a schedule, share the file and import it on the other iPhone.
