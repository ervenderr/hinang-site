Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Hinang does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your data stays on your device

Shifts, shift types, rotations and pay settings are stored locally on your iPhone and are used only by Hinang and its widgets. Hinang makes no network requests and includes no third-party SDKs.

Sharing is your choice

If you export your schedule as a file or calendar (.ics), you decide who receives it through the iOS share sheet. Hinang does not upload it anywhere.

Backups

Your data may be included in your own iPhone backups (for example iCloud Backup), which are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by Hinang.

Children

Hinang does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
